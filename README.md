One picture of one day: a newspaper front page for Tuesday 24 November
2026. The lead story is Ukraine sinking a Russian warship in Libya —
seven Ukrainian sea drones sank the corvette Merkury at Benghazi naval
base in the early hours of Sunday 22 November, confirmed by President
Zelensky on Monday 23 November. The text is honest about the dispute:

Every headline and figure was cross-checked on 24 November 2026
against reporting from Reuters, CNN, AP, the WSJ, Al Jazeera, Defense
News, The Times and Fox News. Weekend and Monday events are dated in
the text. Two stories were deliberately left out: the COP31 collapse
(it led the 22 November edition) and Monday's AI sell-off (it led the
23 November edition; Monday in fact rebounded).

## How it was made

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is drawn by `main.py` — a from-scratch Pillow
program (fixed text, fixed DejaVu fonts, fixed geometry, no network,
no clock) in this repository. Running it twice writes byte-identical
PNGs. It writes to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, or to `./entry.png`
beside `main.py` when the variable is unset.

```sh
python3 main.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Requires Python 3 and Pillow.
